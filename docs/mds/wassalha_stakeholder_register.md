# WASSALHA

**B2B COD Delivery Aggregator for Morocco**

## STAKEHOLDER REGISTER

**Version 1.0 | March 2026**

Classification: Confidential

---

## 1. Purpose

This Stakeholder Register identifies all individuals, groups, and organizations that have an interest in or influence over the Wassalha project. It defines their power, interest, engagement strategy, and communication approach to ensure effective stakeholder management throughout the 8-week MVP sprint.

---

## 2. Power/Interest Grid

Stakeholders are classified using a Power/Interest matrix to determine the appropriate engagement strategy:

|  | **High Interest** | **Low Interest** |
|---|---|---|
| **High Power** | **MANAGE CLOSELY** — S01 Sponsor, S02 PM, S03 Tech Lead, S08 Carriers | **KEEP SATISFIED** — S10 Legal Advisor, S13 Payment Provider |
| **Low Power** | **KEEP INFORMED** — S04-S06 Devs/Mktg, S07 Retailers, S09 API Contacts, S12 Platforms, S15 Beta Testers, S16 Community Leaders | **MONITOR** — S11 Accountant, S14 Cloud Providers |

---

## 3. Engagement Strategy Legend

- **Manage Closely:** High power + high interest. Engage deeply, involve in decisions.
- **Keep Satisfied:** High power + low interest. Keep happy, don't overwhelm with detail.
- **Keep Informed:** Low power + high interest. Regular updates, seek feedback.
- **Monitor:** Low power + low interest. Minimal effort, periodic checks.
